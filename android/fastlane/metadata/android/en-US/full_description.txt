rebirth is a life-restart simulator with a computational-psychiatry model of traumatic memory.

Features:
- Trauma as a dynamical system: memory trace, amygdala and prefrontal control coupled year by year
- Hysteresis: entering a pathological attractor requires a higher load than leaving it
- Heritable stress sensitivity: each life shapes the next generation
- 339 hand-written events, 63 talents, 26 careers, 13 birth backgrounds
- Bilingual: Chinese and English content
- Deterministic seeds: share a seed and replay the exact same life
- Fully offline playable

Optional AI narration:
- Configure your own LLM providers (DeepSeek, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint)
- Multiple providers can be added; they are tried in order as fallbacks
- No provider configured = completely offline, no network requests
- API keys are stored encrypted in the Android Keystore and never leave the device

Content advisory: this game contains mature themes (trauma, mental illness, sex work, cult abuse). Fictional, written for adults, text only.

Privacy: no ads, no trackers, no telemetry. The only permission used is INTERNET, and only when you enable AI narration.
